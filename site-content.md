### Key Content Points

Introduction:

- Bhawana Capital Private Limited, formerly Bhawana Securities and Financial Services Limited, is a non-deposit taking NBFC registered with RBI.
- The company provides short-term loans and advances.
- The FPC is prepared in conformity with RBI Guidelines on Fair Practices Code for NBFCs and approved by the Board.
- The code applies to all product and service categories.

Objectives:

- Promote good practices in customer dealings.
- Promote a fair customer-company relationship.
- Ensure legal compliance in recovery of advances.
- Strengthen customer grievance redressal.

Key commitments:

- Act fairly and reasonably.
- Help customers understand product financial implications.
- Correct mistakes and handle complaints quickly and sympathetically.
- Publicize the code and make copies available on request.

Loan application and processing:

- Offers financial products including instant personal loans through a 100% digital process.
- Communications are in English, with possible borrower language preference.
- Application forms should provide necessary information for comparison and informed decisions.
- Application forms identify required documents.
- Digital acknowledgements are issued for loan applications.
- No discrimination based on caste, creed, or religion.
- Complete applications are processed within a reasonable time.

Loan appraisal and terms:

- Applications are assessed under credit policies and procedures.
- Sanction terms include loan amount, terms, annualized interest rate, and method of application.
- Penal interest for late repayment is disclosed in bold in relevant documents/channels.
- Digital acceptance can include digital signing, Aadhaar OTP, clickwrap, or clickthrough acceptance.
- Terms apply without discrimination.

Disbursement and changes:

- Borrowers are notified of changes to disbursement schedule, interest rates, service charges, prepayment charges, and similar matters.
- Changes are prospective and subject to agreement terms.
- Recall or acceleration follows the loan agreement.
- Securities are released after repayment unless a legitimate right/lien exists.

General:

- Company avoids unnecessary interference in borrower affairs except as allowed by loan terms or customer consent.
- Borrowal account transfer request responses are communicated within 21 days.
- Recovery avoids harassment, odd-hour persistent bothering, or use of muscle power.
- Staff are trained to deal appropriately with customers.
- No discrimination against physically or visually challenged applicants, subject to credit eligibility.

Grievance Redressal:

- Disputes should be heard and disposed of at least at the next higher level.
- Board periodically reviews FPC compliance and grievance mechanism functioning.
- Public notice should include GRO/Nodal Officer details and RBI DoS escalation.

Interest Rate Model:

- Rate and charges are based on cost of equity, borrowed funds, margin, credit risk, operating cost, industry trend, and associated risk.
- Interest rates and risk gradation are disclosed in application/website and sanction communication.
- Rates are annualized.
- Excessive interest rates should not be charged.

Review:

- Board may review and change the Fair Practice Code as required.
- RBI directions and better practices are followed unless conflicting with regulations.

### Elements of Policy

Four key elements:

- Customer Acceptance Policy
- Customer Identification Procedure
- Monitoring of Transactions
- Risk Management

Customer Acceptance Policy:

- No accounts/disbursals in anonymous, fictitious, or benami names.
- CDD must be successful before customer acceptance.
- No transaction/account relationship without CDD.
- Mandatory KYC information collected at onboarding and periodic updates.
- Optional information collected only with explicit consent after account opening.
- Customers acting on behalf of others must be covered by clear stipulations.
- Systems should ensure customer identity does not match RBI-circulated sanctions lists.
- Safeguards should not harass genuine customers or restrict financially/socially disadvantaged segments.

Customer Identification Procedure:

- Uses reliable independent source documents/data/information.
- Verification should establish identity and intended nature of relationship.
- Due diligence should be demonstrable to competent authorities.
- Identification occurs at account opening, when authenticity/adequacy is doubtful, and for financial transactions.
- Unique Customer Identification Code (UCIC) is allotted at onboarding.
